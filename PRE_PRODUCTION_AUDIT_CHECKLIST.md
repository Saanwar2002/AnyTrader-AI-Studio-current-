## Detailed Findings Log

### A. Sign-Up & Onboarding (`Login.tsx`, `AuthProvider.tsx`, `Onboarding.tsx`, `accountBootstrap.ts`, `firestore.rules`)
1. **`paywallEnabled === false` skips trade selection (`Onboarding.tsx:995–1004`):** When free mode is active and `role === "business"`, clicking Continue skips Step 1.5 and Step 2 and submits with empty `businessLayer` and `selectedTrades`, leaving the trader as a `homeowner` with no `public_profiles` record.
2. **Onboarding certification uploads dropped (`Onboarding.tsx:459–475, 1546–1555`, `accountBootstrap.ts:14–30, 194`):** Step 3 reads files with `FileReader.readAsDataURL` (with `https://example.com/docs/...` fallback) instead of uploading to Firebase Storage, and `/api/account/bootstrap-profile` ignores `verificationDocs` and hardcodes `verificationStatus: "unverified"`. Both "I'll do this later" and "Complete Setup" share the same stale-closure submit handler.
3. **Property / Landlord businesses misclassified as Tradespeople (`Onboarding.tsx:1377`, `Profile.tsx:420`, `BillingManager.tsx:733`, `stripeConnectHelpers.ts:11`, `firestore.rules:297`):** Step 2 shows trade categories to property businesses; `role === "business"` grants payout eligibility and trader UI to landlords; `firestore.rules` permits client edits to `businessLayer`.
4. **Role clarity & step indicator (`Onboarding.tsx:691`):** Step 1 lacks Passenger clarity on the customer card and Solo Tradesperson clarity on the trade card; progress pill hardcodes "Step 1 of 3".
5. **Step 2 Back button & fallback mismatch (`Onboarding.tsx:516–538, 1328, 1461`, `accountBootstrap.ts:169–207`, `firestore.rules:256–281`):** Back button skips Step 1.5; client fallback writes fields disallowed by `firestore.rules`; server bootstrap omits `memberId` and `subscriptionType`. External `api64.ipify.org` call and dead `window.confirm`/`alert` code remain.
6. **Login & email verification (`Login.tsx:39–57, 116–164, 558, 588–602`, `AuthProvider.tsx:266–268`):** Unverified emails log straight in with no banner or Resend button; password error says "minimum 6 characters" vs 8-char rule; Terms/Privacy text isn't clickable; Biometric Quick-Login uses a 600ms mock delay and still asks for password.

### B. Profiles, Settings & Stripe Payment Methods (`Profile.tsx`, `BillingManager.tsx`, `HomeownerIdentityCard.tsx`)
1. **Broken Profile Payouts (`Profile.tsx:2708–2743`):** Checks `profile?.stripeConnectId` instead of `profile?.stripeAccountId`; "Set up Payouts" only fires `alert(...)`; "Stripe Dashboard" calls `window.open("https://connect.stripe.com/express/dashboard")` instead of `/api/stripe/create-login-link`; info banner falsely claims separate Stripe accounts are needed for AnyTrader vs AnyRoller.
2. **PCI hazard & simulation copy (`Profile.tsx:3880–3925`):** Tier checkout modal collects raw card number, expiry, and CVC in React state, displays *"This is a simulation environment; no real funds will be moved."*, and then discards the inputs to open Stripe Checkout.
3. **Role-check bugs (`Profile.tsx:659, 741, 891, 961, 1897`, `HomeownerIdentityCard.tsx:65`):** Reviews and quote usage check `role === "tradesperson"` (missing `"business"`); driver checks miss `"fleet_driver"`; homeowner job history hides jobs posted by tradespeople acting as customers.
4. **Saved cards & BillingManager gaps (`BillingManager.tsx:81, 185–192, 306–533, 733–1396`, `Profile.tsx:835–848`, `server.ts:3251–3259, 3339–3341`):** Saved cards hidden from traders/businesses/drivers in Billing; Gold Driver tier shown to non-driver tradespeople; fake `index === 0` default-card badge; card deletion ignores `!res.ok`; customer portal fails with 400 if `stripeCustomerId` isn't created yet.
5. **Dead links & missing settings (`Profile.tsx:1662, 1672, 1679, 1772, 1854, 1860, 1888`):** Multiple menu items point to `/profile` (no-op); browser `alert()`/`confirm()` calls remain; phone number editing and account deletion/GDPR controls need clean profile wiring.

### C. Dual-Role Payment Logic (`pricingCatalog.ts`, `paymentLedger.ts`, `stripeConnectHelpers.ts`, `tripPaymentAuthorization.ts`, `PassengerBooking.tsx`, `DriverMenu.tsx`, `DriverDocuments.tsx`)
1. **Commission & cap mismatch (`BillingManager.tsx:783–828`, `pricingCatalog.ts:355–374`, `paymentLedger.ts:494, 526`):** UI advertises 5% / 3.5% / 2.5% / 1.5%; funding uses 12% default for PAYG + tier rates capped at £250; escrow release & ledger hardcode 12% uncapped—causing ledger drift and overcharging Pro/Gold/Platinum traders on non-destination transfers.
2. **Card-on-file assumption (`PassengerBooking.tsx:1852, 1963, 2007, 4099, 4270`):** Uses `!!profile?.stripeCustomerId` instead of checking real attached saved cards.
3. **Connect entity type (`stripeConnectHelpers.ts:153`):** Hardcodes `business_type: "individual"` instead of letting Ltd companies select their entity type.
4. **Defense-in-depth self-payment check (`pricingCatalog.ts:300–353`, `tripPaymentAuthorization.ts:134–182`):** Should explicitly reject `payerUid === payeeUid`.
5. **AnyRoller driver UI stubs (`DriverMenu.tsx:264–281`, `DriverDocuments.tsx:156`):** Dead Connect buttons, hardcoded `"**** 4242"`, and `https://fake-url.com/` document URLs.
6. **Tier & Add-On Checkout Routing, Upgrades & Cancellation Revocation (Resolved in Task 3B):** `pricingCatalog.ts` previously fell back to Silver Pro (£19.99) whenever `body.tierName` was present without a matching `priceId` (misrouting Exclusive Leads, Video Pro, Ad Wallet Top-Up, and Gotham SaaS); paid-to-paid upgrades created duplicate Stripe subscriptions instead of updating in place; Founding PRO £9.99/mo was charged £19.99/mo; Landlord Pro shared `subscriptionId` with Trader tiers and was cleared on Trader checkout; cancelled subscriptions left stale `tier`/`tierId`/`isLandlord` entitlements active; Appointments add-on (£9.99/mo) had no Stripe wiring; `BillingManager.tsx` Free Explorer called Checkout instead of `/api/select-free-tier`; and `public_profiles` was not synced with tier/Video Pro updates. All resolved and verified by unit tests.
